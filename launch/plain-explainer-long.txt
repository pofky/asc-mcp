You made a toy and you want it in a giant toy store, the App Store. The store has a strict office. Before your toy reaches the shelf, you fill out lots of forms, add pictures, set a price, and wait for a helper to check it all. Forget one thing and they say "no, try again", and days are gone.

asc-mcp is a helper robot for that office. It talks to your AI assistant, so you just say what you want. "How is my toy doing?" It tells you whether the store said yes or no, and with Pro, what people wrote about your toy. "Is my toy ready?" It checks your forms first, like a teacher reading your homework before you hand it in: "You forgot a picture, and this link is broken." "Fix it for me." It fills in forms, adds pictures and sets prices, and before a big move, like changing a price or handing your toy to the store, it stops and asks: are you sure?

Checking whether the store said yes or no is free, with no time limit. Everything else works free for 7 days, no card, then it's $9 a month, VAT included, with sales tax added in the US, Canada and India. The robot checks your homework, and the store still decides.

For the grown-ups: the toy is your app, the office is App Store Connect, and the robot is an MCP server running on your own computer. https://asc-mcp.pages.dev
