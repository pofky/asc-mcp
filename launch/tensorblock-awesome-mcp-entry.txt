- [asc-mcp](https://github.com/pofky/asc-mcp): Lets an agent ship an iOS app through the App Store Connect API, from a new bundle id to App Review: registers the bundle id and capabilities, creates the App Store signing profile, archives and uploads the build with Xcode, sets up TestFlight groups and testers, writes metadata, age rating and screenshots, creates in-app purchases and subscriptions, audits the version before submission, then submits and releases. 45 tools, 6 slash-command prompts and a Claude Skill. Writes ask for `confirm: true` first. Runs locally; the `.p8` key never leaves the machine. Six tools are free; the rest have a 7-day trial with no card, then $9/month. Install: `npx -y @pofky/asc-mcp`. Stdio transport. Official MCP Registry id `io.github.pofky/asc-mcp`. MIT.
